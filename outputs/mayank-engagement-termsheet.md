## 3. Intellectual Property — *Critical Section*

### 3.1 Definitions

| Category | Definition | Ownership |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-Existing IP (Vijay's)** | Code, modules, libraries, frameworks, designs, or know-how created or owned by Vijay/Fracktal prior to the engagement or developed independently during the engagement | **Vijay / Fracktal Works** — sole and exclusive |
| **Pre-Existing IP (Mayank's)** | Domain data, specifications, trade secrets, customer lists provided by Mayank | **Mayank / His Entity** |
| **New IP — Developed for Mayank** | Software, code, and documentation created from scratch specifically for Mayank's project requirements, not derived from Vijay's Pre-Existing IP | **Assigned to Mayank's entity upon full payment** — Vijay retains a perpetual, royalty-free license for internal use and for use in non-competing products |
| **Derivative / Mixed IP** | Modifications, extensions, or integrations that combine Vijay's Pre-Existing IP with new work for Mayank | **Vijay owns the underlying Pre-Existing IP + modifications to it.** Mayank receives a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use (not resell or sublicense) the combined work for his business operations |

### 3.2 Licensing Model (Recommended)

Instead of IP assignment, structure as a **license**:

- Mayank's entity receives: **Perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license** to use, modify, and deploy the software for his own business operations and for his end-customers.
- Mayank's entity does NOT receive: The right to resell, sublicense, or white-label the software as a standalone product to third parties (unless negotiated separately with additional compensation).
- Vijay retains: Full ownership of all Pre-Existing IP, the right to license the same or similar software to customers in other verticals or geographies, and the right to use generalized learnings (not Mayank's confidential data) in future work.

### 3.3 The "Already Built It" Scenario

If Mayank requests software that Vijay has already developed (in whole or part):

1. Vijay discloses that prior work exists (without revealing source code or customer details).
2. The parties agree on one of:
   - **License fee model:** Mayank pays a one-time or recurring license fee to use the existing software, with customization billed separately.
   - **Revenue share on that module only:** Mayank pays a lower upfront fee + a percentage of revenue generated using that module.
   - **Full purchase / assignment:** Mayank buys the IP outright at a negotiated valuation (recommended only at a significant premium, given Vijay's opportunity cost of not licensing to others).
3. In all cases, **Vijay retains the right to continue licensing the original, unmodified software to other customers in non-competing domains.**

## 4. Audit & Verification Rights

> *Non-negotiable for any profit-share arrangement.*

- **Quarterly statements:** Mayank provides a statement of revenue and attributable profit for each software product within 30 days of quarter end.
- **Annual audit right:** Vijay (or his designated CA) may inspect relevant financial records once per calendar year, with 15 days written notice, during business hours.
- **Cost:** If audit reveals underreporting of 5% or more, Mayank bears audit cost. Otherwise, Vijay bears it.
- **Trigger events:** Automatic reporting when a new customer goes live, when a contract exceeding ₹[threshold] is signed, or when monthly active users cross defined thresholds.
- **Confidentiality:** All financial data reviewed under audit is confidential and used solely for verification.

## 5. Scope of Work & Change Management

### 5.1 Statement of Work (SOW)

Every project begins with a written SOW (can be a 1-2 page document) covering:

- Project description and objectives
- Deliverables with acceptance criteria
- Timeline with milestones
- Payment schedule tied to milestones
- Assumptions and dependencies (e.g., Mayank provides domain data, customer access, etc.)
- What is explicitly OUT of scope

### 5.2 Change Orders

- Any change to scope, timeline, or deliverables triggers a **Change Order** — a short written amendment to the SOW.
- Vijay provides an estimate of additional cost and time impact.
- Work on changes does not begin until the Change Order is mutually agreed in writing (email sufficient).
- This protects against scope creep and "while you're at it" additions.

## 8. Termination

### 8.1 Termination for Convenience

- Either party may terminate with [30-60] days written notice.
- On termination:
  - All outstanding invoices become immediately due.
  - Vijay delivers all completed work and work-in-progress up to the termination date.
  - Licenses granted up to that point survive termination.

### 8.2 Termination for Cause

- Either party may terminate immediately if the other:
  - Materially breaches the agreement and fails to cure within 15 days of written notice
  - Becomes insolvent or enters liquidation
- Non-payment beyond 30 days = material breach by Mayank.

### 8.3 Effect on IP Licenses

- **Critical clause:** All licenses granted to Mayank for Pre-Existing IP and Derivative IP **survive termination** as long as all outstanding payments are settled. If payments are not settled, licenses are suspended until payment.

## 9. Source Code Access & Escrow

- **During engagement:** Vijay maintains the canonical repository. Mayank has read access to project repositories.
- **On termination (with all payments settled):** Mayank receives a complete copy of all New IP source code, build scripts, and documentation — sufficient for another competent developer to maintain and extend the software.
- **Pre-Existing IP source code:** NOT delivered. Mayank receives compiled libraries/APIs and integration documentation only, unless a full source-code license is separately negotiated and paid for.
- **Escrow option:** Source code for New IP may be placed with a neutral third party (lawyer or escrow service) with release conditions tied to Vijay's death, incapacity, or material breach.

## Appendix: Key Principles (Your North Star)

1. **Paid for time, rewarded for outcomes** — retainer covers your existence; profit share is upside, not a substitute for payment.
2. **Your IP is your moat** — never assign what you can license. Once you give away IP, you can't get it back.
3. **Trust is earned through transparency** — audit rights aren't hostile; they're how trust scales beyond handshake deals.
4. **Written scope = freedom** — clear boundaries let you say "yes" to the next thing without guilt.
5. **The existing project is the past; everything new is a fresh negotiation.**
